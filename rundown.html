10:0012min

Opening: The Four Moves

10:002 min Land the session
Say

Monday we talked about color and light. Today we talk about the camera, and about the fact that it moves.

By the time you leave you will be able to name what a camera is doing, and say whether it had a reason to do it.

Do
  • Write four words on the board and leave them up: PAN, TILT, DOLLY, CRANE.
10:027 min The four moves, on your own body
Say

Pan is my head turning. Tilt is my head nodding. Dolly is me walking toward you. Crane is me being lifted.

That is the entire vocabulary for today and you already own all of it in your body.

Do
  • Demonstrate each one physically. Turn, nod, walk toward the front row, then reach up.
  • Make them do it with you. It takes thirty seconds and it is the reason they remember.

10:093 min The question that runs all day
Say

One question runs through everything today. Not what is the camera doing. What is the camera chasing.

A move that is chasing something is motivated. A move that is chasing nothing is decoration. By 12:45 you will be able to tell them apart inside ninety seconds of footage.

Do
  • Write WHAT IS IT CHASING? on the board. Leave it up for the whole session.
  • Do not define motivated formally yet. They earn it at 10:48.

Watch it once. I am not going to say anything over it.

Show Spider-Man: Into the Spider-Verse, Official Trailer Sony Pictures Entertainment · 2:40 · 10:12 · play 1:00 to 1:30, then again whole Only two minutes forty, so you can afford to stop on every move. This is the anchor text.
10:2012 min Spider-Verse, second pass, stop on every move
Say

Freeze. What just happened to the frame?

Good. Now the second half. What was it chasing?

Do
  • Pause constantly. Cold-call by name, alternate around the room.
  • When the frame tilts, name Dutch angle. When the layers slide, name parallax.
  • Watch for the moment the camera stops behaving like a camera and starts behaving like the character. That is the one to slow down on.

10:426 min The comparison, which is the block
Say

Which of those two cameras is freer?

Now the harder one. Which of those two cameras means more?

I shipped football games at EA where the broadcast camera barely moved, and that was a design decision, not a limitation.

Do
  • Let the second question sit. Do not answer it for them.
  • SAY THE EA LINE. You consistently undersell this and it is the single thing in the room that makes the argument concrete: you have actually shipped a camera.
10:4818min

Activity: Movement Annotation

10:483 min Set up the annotation
Say

Two columns on your page. Left column, the move. Right column, one sentence saying what that move is chasing.

If you cannot write the sentence, still write the move down. Those are the interesting ones.

Do
  • Put the two column headings on the board: MOVE / WHAT IT IS CHASING.
  • Ninety seconds of footage. Tell them it will loop and they do not need to rush.
Show Grand Theft Auto VI, Trailer 1 Rockstar Games · 1:30 · 10:48 · the assigned clip, played on a loop Loop it. Do not play it once. This is the clip the whole activity hangs on.

Second Text: What a Real Camera Costs

11:163 min Frame the second text
Say

You have spent an hour in worlds where moving the camera is free. A crane shot costs a keyframe.

Now watch somebody talk about a camera you have to carry.

Do
  • Do not preview the argument. Let Coogler make it.
11:193 min Play it
Do
  • Play the whole two minutes. It is short.
Show Sinners: Ryan Coogler on Shooting With IMAX Film Cameras IMAX · 2:02 · 11:16 · play whole, then discuss Two minutes carrying thirty. Questions ready before you press play.
11:2212 min What changes when the camera is heavy
Say

What changes about a director's choices when the camera weighs something?

Do
  • Draw out: weight, setup time, a magazine that runs out, a crew that has to be paid.
  • Name focal length when aspect ratio comes up. Format and lens are the same kind of decision: made before the shot, and it decides what the shot can mean.
Term · Also covered Focal length How wide or narrow the lens sees. Wide lenses exaggerate depth and distance between planes; long lenses compress them and flatten the space. Teaches itWhat is Lens Compression? Camera Focal Length Explained Remy Leonard · 5:29 Seen inCoogler on Shooting With IMAX Film Cameras
11:3412 min Turn it around, which is the point
Say

Your engine camera weighs nothing. Nothing at all.

So what stops you from moving it constantly? And what is lost when nothing does?

Do
  • This is the block. Give it the full twelve minutes.
  • If the room goes quiet, ask them to name one move from the annotation they would cut first if it cost ten thousand dollars.
11:4625min

Annotation Share-Back

11:463 min Pair up
Say

Find someone who is not sitting next to you. Trade pages.

11:4912 min Compare annotations
Say

Where you both marked the same move and wrote different sentences, stop there. That disagreement is the useful part, not a mistake.

Do
  • Circulate. Do not resolve the disagreements for them.
  • Do not let a confident student settle one by volume. Ask the quieter one to say theirs again.
12:0110 min Board the two columns
Say

Which moves did nobody manage a sentence for?

Do
  • Two columns on the board: SURVIVED A SENTENCE / DID NOT.
  • The second column is the whole lesson. Let it get long and do not rescue it.
