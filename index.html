Second Text, Sinners, and What a Real Camera Costs
You have spent an hour naming moves in animation and in engines, where a crane shot costs nothing but a keyframe. Now watch Coogler talk about shooting on IMAX film. Every move he describes has a price: weight, setup time, a magazine that runs out. Ask the room what changes about a director's choices when the camera is heavy. Then turn it around, because that is the point of this block. Your engine camera weighs nothing, so what stops you from moving it constantly, and what is lost when nothing does.

Terms for today

Depth of FieldThe zone of sharp focus in an image; controlled by aperture, focal length, and distance. Deep DoF keeps foreground and background sharp; shallow DoF isolates subject.
Dutch AngleA tilted or canted frame, not level with the horizon; often signals unease, chaos, or subjective perspective.
Pan/Tilt/BoomCamera movements: pan (horizontal turn), tilt (vertical turn), boom (vertical lift). Boom is also a microphone arm.
ParallaxThe apparent shift in position between foreground and background layers when camera moves; creates depth and dimensionality.
